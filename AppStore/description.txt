Curio Case — a private catalog for what you collect.

Organize pins, vinyl, shells, coins, miniatures, ticket stubs, and other collections on your own device.

- Create, rename, and delete collections.
- Define custom text fields for each collection.
- Add and edit items with optional photos, condition, tags, notes, acquisition date, and a value you declare yourself.
- Search item names, tags, notes, and custom fields across collections; filter by collection and condition.
- Use the catalog without attaching any photos. Camera and gallery access are requested only when you choose to attach a photo.

Your catalog and imported photo copies stay in app-local storage. There are no accounts, cloud services, ads, or analytics, and the app makes no network requests. Declared values are your own records, not appraisals.

Built with Flutter for phones, tablets, and Windows desktop, using Cupertino controls on iOS, Material controls on Android, and Fluent controls on Windows.

Backups, exports, location management, and collection dashboards are planned for later releases. Removing the app may remove its local catalog and photos.
