Curio Case — the quiet catalog for everything you collect.

Enamel pins, vinyl, shells, coins, teapots, minis, ticket stubs — your collections live in boxes and drawers, not in someone else's cloud. Curio Case gives every collection its own shelf: items with photos, condition notes, a storage location you choose ("Studio > Shelf B > Tin 3"), tags, and custom fields that fit each hobby.

WHAT IT DOES
- Create unlimited collections, each with its own fields and templates
- Add items in seconds: photo, name, condition, location, tags, optional notes and dates
- Find anything instantly with search, tag filters, and sorting
- Track where things live with a location tree and per-item move history
- Watch your hobby grow with collection stats: counts, condition spread, acquisitions per year, and an optional declared-value total you enter yourself
- Export a CSV catalog or a full JSON backup whenever you want, and restore it on any device you own

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Curio Case makes zero network requests. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, no analytics, and no cloud. Camera and photo access are only asked for when you add a photo — and every feature works without photos. Your collection data belongs to you, on your devices.

Works on phones, tablets, and Windows desktop with layouts and controls that feel native on each: iOS navigation, Material on Android, Fluent on Windows. Touch, keyboard, and mouse all supported.

This description reflects the planned app; features ship incrementally with each release.
